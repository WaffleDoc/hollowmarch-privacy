Hollowmarch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Hollowmarch is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Hollowmarch does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no online features other than optional purchases through Google Play.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google may share a purchase token with the game so it can deliver what you bought; it stays on your device.

Your save

Your progress is stored only on your device, in the game's local storage. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. The optional save backup code in Settings is text you copy yourself; it never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on Reminders in Settings, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when an expedition returns). Nothing is sent over the internet.
  • Keep awake / run after restart: lets the screen stay on during auto battles and lets scheduled reminders survive a phone restart.
  • Billing: used only by Google Play to process optional purchases.

Hollowmarch does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or any other personal information.

Children

Hollowmarch collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds any online feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
